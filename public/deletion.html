Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: May 13, 2026

AD SCALE respects your right to request deletion of personal data and data obtained through Meta/Facebook integrations, according to applicable privacy laws and Meta Platform Policy.

How to request deletion

You may request complete deletion of your data through the channel below:

Email:
Send an email to adscalecontingencia@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request", including:
  1. Full name
  2. Email registered on the platform
  3. Meta/Facebook user ID, if applicable
Platform account:
Access your account and contact support to request account deletion.

What will be deleted

  • Registration data such as name, email, phone, and account credentials.
  • Financial and transaction history associated with your account.
  • Meta/Facebook tokens and synchronized data such as business assets, accounts, pages, and insights.
  • Access logs and notifications.

Timeline

Your request will be processed within 30 days. Once deleted, the data cannot be recovered.

Exceptions

We may retain information strictly required to comply with legal obligations for the minimum period required by law.

Confirmation

You will receive an email confirming completion of the deletion request.

Contact

Data Protection Contact: adscalecontingencia@gmail.com
